# Dens

Self-hosted chat app with text, voice and screen share for your friends or a small community.  
It runs on your own computer, or a friend's, instead of a company's servers.

- **Get started:**
  [install Dens](https://dens.chat/docs/install/), then
  [join a den](https://dens.chat/docs/usage/) or
  [host one](https://dens.chat/docs/hosting/).
- **Privacy:** "ambient privacy, local-first, credible exit".  
  DMs, group DMs, calls and screen shares are end-to-end encrypted, and
  the den can read what's in its channels: [who can read what](https://dens.chat/docs/privacy/).
- **Platforms:** Linux and Windows 11, for both joining and hosting dens.
  A Flatpak and an Android app, for joining them.
