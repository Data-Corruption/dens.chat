# Dens — Milestones

What each milestone built, and the plan for the one under way; ideas no milestone has taken up yet are in [loose-ideas.md](loose-ideas.md). The design they build toward is [design.md](design.md), with each deployment form's in [installed.md](installed.md), [flatpak.md](flatpak.md) and [android.md](android.md): a section named here, such as Global shortcuts, is one of theirs unless it's one of this file's.

A milestone's plan is written here first, and reviewed before anything is built. Once the milestone is done, what its plan still teaches, such as spike findings and measurements, moves into the design docs' sections, and the rest goes; Git keeps it.

Each milestone ends usable on its own and is tested on Linux and Windows, in Chromium and Firefox. As a rule, each step of a milestone is tested by the automated checks: the harnesses, the Android e2e in the emulator, and the browser checks in the container's Chromium and Firefox. The manual tests, on the phones and in browsers, run together once, at the milestone's end. A spike runs its own trials, and a test that can't wait for the end gets an exception. Platform-specific code stays behind the seams AGENTS.md's "OS seams live in two places" names, so the rest of a milestone is shared code.

M23.1:

- *Tailwind's sources.* `input.css` imports Tailwind with `source(none)`, and names what holds the page's classes: `internal/ui/src` and `internal/ui/templates`. A class a script builds from parts, which a scan of the sources can't find, is named whole in `input.css`. It goes first, since until it lands this plan's words are among what Tailwind reads. The step compares `output.css` before and after, and searches the page's sources for each class a dropped rule names.
- *A dialog's sentence.* Remove's and Ban's says "They're signed out of this den at once", as its other branch does, and [The page's words](design/page.md#the-pages-words) gains the rule: a dialog about keys and codes names nobody, and one about a member names them by @username alone. The step reads every dialog's sentences for a display name.
- *Voice's keys.* `VoiceSettings` reads the keys and the helper again in its effect, as `NotificationSettings` does, with a page test that changes them between its render and its effect.
- *The checklist.* [security.md](design/security.md) gains sections for identities, group DMs, notes to self, spoilers, events and badges, and call records, each item written from its design and checked against the code as it's written, with a probe where a claim needs one. An item that doesn't hold is fixed in the step where the fix is small, and raised where it isn't.
- *What the checklist found* is fixed in the step, and goes out ahead of the rest as v1.14.1: a channel's ID held to its kind for good, a hold that stands under a key the other side made, a trimmed record keeping the member's own identities, and a spoiler's card covered. Three items that don't hold yet stay out of the checklist and wait in [loose-ideas.md](loose-ideas.md#probably-m24), first in M24.

M23.2:

- *Opening it.* Options: (a) a click on a voice channel joins its call, as now, and opens its pane, and a click on the channel the member is in opens it again; (b) a click opens the pane, which lists who's there, and its Join joins; (c) a click joins and leaves the page where it is, as now, and a second opens the pane. Pick (a): joining stays one click, a member outside a call may watch none of its shares, so (b)'s pane would show what the list's rows already do, and (c)'s pane waits behind a click nobody knows to make. It takes the member off the channel they were reading, which Back returns to. The pane is the member's own call's alone. An event's Join, a LIVE mark's join and the switcher's Enter open it as the row's click does; a join the member didn't ask for, staff's move or the page joining again after a den restart, leaves the page where it is.
- *Its address* is the voice channel's, `/den/<den>/<channel>`, a level as a text channel's is, so Back and the phone's panes take it as one. A page at that address with no call there, as after a reload, which leaves the call, or once the call ends, shows the channel last shown.
- *Tiles.* Each member is a tile: their picture, their name in its style, their marks, and the speaking ring around it. Its ⋯, a right click or a long press opens the menu their row has. A call of 15 fits a window of 1280 by 800 without scrolling. The list keeps its rows.
- *Shares.* A share is a tile marked LIVE until the member watches it, which stays a click, since each watcher counts against the share's viewers and the den's upload. Watched, it takes the stage, the members' tiles a row under it, and two shares stand side by side, drawn by the player's own tile. While the pane shows, the floating player shows nothing, as it does on the phone layout, and it takes the shares back as the member leaves for a text channel. A share in a window of its own stays there.
- *Foot and header.* The foot holds the call's controls, labeled: share, mute, deafen, Away, the soundboard, the Voice settings and leave. The header holds the channel's name, how the call stands while it isn't connected, the lock, and the call's length where Call timer is on.
- *The block.* Options: (a) it stays under the channel list while the pane shows, without its cog; (b) it hides while the pane shows. Pick (a): it's where the call is from every other screen, and controls that come and go under the pointer are worse than controls shown twice. The Voice settings open from the pane's foot and from the settings.
- *The phone layout.* The pane is the voice channel's pane there, and a tap on the call's bar opens it, where it opened the den's list. Options for a share while the member reads elsewhere: (a) the docked player stays above a text channel, as the floating player stays on a wide window, and shows nothing while the pane shows; (b) it goes, and a share shows in the pane alone. Pick (a): a member watches and chats on a phone too, and a share's sound plays from its `<video>`, which (b) would have to keep playing out of sight.
- *Away.* A toggle in the pane's foot and in the block's grid. The page mutes the member, as Mute does, and tells the den (`voice.mute` gains `away`, and `voice.join` beside its `muted` and `deafened`, so a move keeps it), which marks them in `voice.state` for everyone who sees the call: z's on their row and their tile, and Away in the tip. Options for coming back: (a) the toggle alone; (b) the toggle, and unmuting or pressing push to talk, by any key, button or helper. Pick (b): a member who talks is back, and a mark that outlasts that is wrong. Nothing sets it from the page going quiet, since a member in a game leaves the page untouched for hours. The den keeps it with the call, in memory, through a hold. A den whose info doesn't say `away` gets no toggle, and an older Dens shows the member muted.
- *As built.* The pane's level opens above the channel shown, so Back returns to it, as Opening it says, and takes another call's pane's place. The stage with two shares is read in `two-shares`, which has two, and `calls` reads the tiles and Away; the checks that read the block, the bar or the player go back from the pane first, as leaving for a text channel does. The pane has no member list beside it. The block's line for a call in another den opens the pane, as the bar does. On the phone layout why a join failed shows above the channel's pane, since the click opened the pane and the block is off screen. The toggle puts the microphone back as it was before Away, as hearing again does after deafen, and a join of the member's own ends Away, so a member who left while away starts their next call as they were before it.

M23.3:

- *The switcher.* Ctrl+K opens it, and so does a magnifier atop a den's list and in Home's header, since a touch screen has no Ctrl; search opens from there when it comes. It's a dialog, a sheet on the phone layout: a box, and under it the channels, DMs and groups of every den this install is in, and the dens, matched as completion matches a member, by the start of a name or of a word in it. Those with something unread come first, the open den's before the others', and before anything is typed it lists the unread alone. ↑, ↓ and Enter open one, a voice channel's Enter joining as a click does. The open den's come from its view, and another den's from its state on this computer (`/api/dens/<den>/state`), fetched as the switcher opens, as a link's chip fetches it; so it lists only what the member sees, and an offline den's as its Dens kept them.
- *Keys.* The page gets one listener for its keys: Ctrl+K; Alt+↑ and Alt+↓, to the channel or DM before and after in the list, passing voice channels; Ctrl+/, the list of keys; and Esc, which with nothing open to close and the message box empty marks the channel read and goes to its newest message. Options where a member bound a voice key to one of them: (a) the voice key takes the press, as it does while they type; (b) the settings refuse the binding. Pick (a): the binding is theirs, and the list says which key it took. Firefox opens its search bar on Ctrl+K unless the page takes the key, as a page may, so the check presses it in both browsers.
- *The list of keys* is a dialog, Keyboard shortcuts: getting around, the composer's, a message's, the viewer's, and the voice keys as the member bound them. Formatting help keeps the composer's and links it. The page offers it where it has keys, so not on a touch screen.
- *Mark as read.* A channel's menu, staff's alone until now, opens for every member, and on a DM's row, by its ⋯, a right click or a long press: Mark as read, then M23.4's Notifications, then Copy link, then staff's rows. The client sends the channel's `last_message_id`, which it keeps, as the read position, so the den changes nothing for it. The den's name's menu gains Mark all as read. Options: (a) a request a channel, where forty pass the member's 30 writes in 10 seconds; (b) `POST /api/read`, which moves the position of every channel and DM the member sees to its newest in one write. Pick (b), offered where the den's info says `read_all`. Either takes back the notifications that reading takes back.
- *Links to channels.* A channel's name is 1 to 32 characters of free text and needn't be the only one, so a message can't hold `#name`. Options: (a) `#name`, read by each page against the den's channels, which fails on two channels of one name, a name with a space, and a rename; (b) a token, as `<#41>`, a new rule in the den's parser and the page's, which notifications, Copy text and an older Dens show as written; (c) the channel's link, `https://den.example.com/#c/<den ID>/41`, beside `#m/` and `#e/`. Pick (c): a message's link already draws the chip, `#general`, checks it at a click and says `#unknown` for a channel out of sight, and a channel's outlasts a rename and works from a DM and from another den. Typing `#` and a character where a mention may start completes the text channels the member sees, by the start of a name or of a word in it, and puts the link in, which the box shows as its address until it's sent, as it shows a message's. The chip shows wherever a message's link's does, and a channel's menu's Copy link copies it.
- *The processor's silence.* The voice processor takes a block with no channels, as Firefox gives for a disabled track, as silence, so its gate, what it sends and the settings' meter go on. Without it the meter freezes, which `talk` in Firefox fails on, on main too.

While it's on nothing shows and no message sounds, an event's start among them; unread marks and the tab's dot go on; and what arrived doesn't show once it ends, as what arrives while a den is out of reach doesn't. What already shows stays.
- *Its end.* For an hour, Until tomorrow, which is the next 8:00 by the device's clock, or Until I turn it off. The page sends the end as a time, so the `notify` check sets one a few seconds off by the same request. The service's configuration keeps it, or that it has none, and reads the wall clock at each message and each event's start. No timer ends it, since Go's stop while a phone sleeps.
- *Where.* Options: (a) a switch atop the settings' Notifications; (b) that, and a moon beside the cog in the member's panel and in Home's header, which opens the three ends and is lit while it's on, its tip saying until when. Pick (b): three presses deep isn't one switch.
- *Whose.* Options: (a) this install's; (b) the member's, kept by the den for all their devices. Pick (a): the den hears nothing of it, and nothing of the den changes. Every page paired with the install shares it, as it shares the kinds, and the member's other devices notify as they're set.

M23.5:

- *Members.* Manage's Members, for staff, above Invites: every member in a table, their names, role, joined and last seen, sorted by a click on a column's head; a box that narrows it by a few letters of a username or a display name; and on each row the menu the member's card has. It reads the view's members, so only last seen is new. On the phone layout a row is the names over the rest.
- *Last seen.* `den_devices.last_seen_at` is written as a device signs in, and a session renews on its socket for as long as that stays up, so an install on for a month would read as a month gone. Options: (a) no last seen; (b) the den writes it at each renewal too, and answers staff with each member's latest, by day (`GET /api/members/seen`, where its info says `members_seen`). Pick (b): finding who hasn't been on in a year is what the list is for, the den holds it already, and a day says less than presence does. A member who's online reads Online. It stays out of `ready`, and Devices' line reads last seen, which it then is.
- *What the log lists.* Staff's acts in the den, a line each: who, what, on whom or where, and when. A member removed or banned, with whether their messages and their invite went; a ban lifted; a role changed, the owner's by an owner invite among them; an invite made or revoked; a channel or a group made, changed or deleted; another member's message deleted, or its reactions taken off; a member disconnected, muted or moved in a call; a call's record reset; an emoji or a sound added, renamed or deleted; another member's event changed, canceled or ended; and the den's name, a limit or retention changed, with the value before and after. Never a message's text, and nothing of a DM, where staff have no powers. A name as it was, a deleted channel's among them, is sealed with the data key, as an event's text is.
- *A deleted message's author.* The den keeps of a deleted message its ID and channel, "never its text, author or files" ([Text and replies](design/messages.md#text-and-replies)). Options for staff deleting another member's: (a) the line names whose it was; (b) it names the channel alone. Pick (a): whose message staff deleted is what the log is asked, and that line of messages.md gains the exception.
- *Members' own deletions* aren't listed: a member's delete leaves what it leaves now, the message's ID and channel. Listing them waits in loose-ideas.md, with the rest of moderation.
- *How long.* Options: (a) for good; (b) 90 days, the den's timer sweeping what's older; (c) the owner's setting. Pick (b): long enough to ask who banned someone, and a den that collects less doesn't keep staff's every act for good. A setting waits for owners to ask.
- *A member who leaves* keeps their record, as their messages keep a name, so lines by them and about them read until their time passes.
- *Reading it.* Manage's Audit log, for moderators and the owner, newest first, a page at a time (`GET /api/audit`), where the den's info says `audit_log`. Nothing of it goes over the socket. Its table is M23's migration, and the den's export and backups carry it.
