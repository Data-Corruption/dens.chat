# Dens — Milestones

What each milestone built, and the plan for the one under way; ideas no milestone has taken up yet are in [loose-ideas.md](loose-ideas.md). The design they build toward is [design.md](design.md), with each deployment form's in [installed.md](installed.md), [flatpak.md](flatpak.md) and [android.md](android.md): a section named here, such as Global shortcuts, is one of theirs unless it's one of this file's.

A milestone's plan is written here first, and reviewed before anything is built. Once the milestone is done, what its plan still teaches, such as spike findings and measurements, moves into the design docs' sections, and the rest goes; Git keeps it.

Each milestone ends usable on its own and is tested on Linux and Windows, in Chromium and Firefox. As a rule, each step of a milestone is tested by the automated checks: the harnesses, the Android e2e in the emulator, and the browser checks in the container's Chromium and Firefox. The manual tests, on the phones and in browsers, run together once, at the milestone's end. A spike runs its own trials, and a test that can't wait for the end gets an exception. Platform-specific code stays behind the seams AGENTS.md's "OS seams live in two places" names, so the rest of a milestone is shared code.

**M25 picks.**

- **Channel roles — visibility only, staff always see everything.** Options: visibility only with staff seeing all channels; owner able to hide channels from moderators; roles also adding badges or permissions. Pick: *visibility only, staff always see everything*, because moderators already manage channels, events, files and the audit log, and members already have Name styles. A channel has one visibility mode: Everyone, Staff only (`staff_only = 1`), or Selected roles (`role_ids` non-empty, visible to staff plus members holding any listed role).
- **Who manages roles.** Options: staff (owner and moderators) manage roles and assignments; split (owner creates/deletes, moderators assign); owner only. Pick: *staff manage everything*, matching channel and event management; every create, rename, delete, member role change and channel role change writes an audit line.
- **Role deletion fails closed.** Options: refuse deletion while used by a channel; flip single-role channels to Everyone; flip single-role channels to Staff only. Pick: *flip single-role channels (and ended events whose only role was deleted) to Staff only*, warning staff in the delete confirmation which channels become Staff only, so deleting a role never exposes a private channel.
- **Role names, visibility and `@role` mentions.** Options: defer `@role` mentions and keep hidden roles private; staff-only `@role` mentions; mentionable roles for everyone. Pick: *defer `@role` mentions and keep hidden roles private*. Role names stay free-form (`1..32` runes, case-insensitively unique, capped at 50 per den), `@everyone` in a role-gated channel already pings everyone who sees that channel, and non-staff snapshots carry only role names and member role IDs for roles on channels the viewer sees (enough for `@` completion and voice drag-moves without leaking hidden role names).
- **Voice channels and events under roles.** Follow `staff_only`'s existing seams: losing a role or restricting a voice channel immediately ends an affected member's call (`VoiceForbidden`); an event in a role-gated voice channel shares its channel's visibility for its card, banner, badge and interested count, snapshotting `den_event_roles` if the channel is deleted.
- **Hub fan-out (confirmed in M25.0).** Options: dense-slot `uint64` bitmask (fits the 50-role cap in one integer, single-instruction `sub.staff || (a.roles & sub.roles) != 0`); sorted `[]int64` intersection; `map[int64]struct{}` per sub. Pick: *dense-slot `uint64` bitmask with targeted refreshes*. On a 500-member den (10 staff, 490 non-staff holding 1–5 of 50 roles) and 10k-event ring (`internal/den/hub_bench_test.go`, Ryzen 7 9800X3D):
  - Pure 10k audience checks (`BenchmarkRoleAudienceIncludes10k`): bitmask `26.2 µs` (`2.6 ns/check`, 0 allocs) vs. sorted slice `52.8 µs` (2.0× slower) vs. map `116.7 µs` (4.5× slower).
  - `Publish` (500 subs, 1 / 3 / 10 roles): bitmask `5.0 / 5.5 / 10.9 µs` (`621–850 B/op`, 2 allocs) vs. sorted slice `5.0 / 6.1 / 11.4 µs` vs. map `5.8 / 11.2 / 26.2 µs` (up to 2.4× slower); packing `staff bool` beside `kind` keeps `Audience` at 48 bytes with zero heap allocation per role audience.
  - `SubscribeAt` (10k-event mixed ring replay): bitmask `235 µs` (`0.24 ms`) vs. sorted slice `250 µs` vs. map `276 µs`.
  - Targeted refreshes (500 subs): channel role change `3.68 µs`, member role change `3.15 µs`, role delete `11.37 µs` vs. `RefreshNonStaff` `13.34 µs` (3.6× faster, avoiding ~450 unnecessary full snapshots).
  - *Refresh & resume rules:* `Everyone ↔ *` channel transitions refresh all non-staff (`nonStaffRefresh`); restricted-to-restricted (`StaffOnly ↔ Roles` or `Roles → Roles`) refreshes only `!sub.staff && (sub.roles & (oldMask | newMask)) != 0` and bumps `roleRefresh[slot]` for each bit in `oldMask | newMask`. `SetMemberRoles` updates `sub.roles`, refreshes non-staff sockets, and sets `memberRefresh[member]` so offline role losses cannot resume with `roles = 0`. `DeleteRole` loads affected member IDs from the DB before deletion, clears the deleted bit on live sockets, sets `memberRefresh` for all former holders (online and offline) and `roleRefresh` for roles sharing any channel with the deleted role, and quarantines the bit slot in `retiredAt[slot] = seq`; `EnsureRole` reuses a slot freely once `retiredAt[slot] < first`, or calls `RefreshNonStaff` first if all 64 slots are active or still in the ring (staff ignore role masks on replay).
- **Forward's link back — cross-den by `den_id` only.** Options: carry `(den_id, channel_id, message_id)` across dens with no hostname; same-den links only; opt-in checkbox in Forward. Pick: *carry `(den_id, channel_id, message_id)` with no URL or hostname*. Friends in both dens get a clickable `Den A · #channel` chip; outsiders see `Forwarded from another den` (and `Forwarded from another channel` for hidden/deleted channels in a joined den, never querying the den). DM and Notes forwards never carry a link; a channel forward into a DM or Notes seals `forward_source` inside `DMPayload`; re-forwarding a channel forward points to the channel message forwarded. Document the cross-den `den_id` correlation in [security.md](design/security.md) and `docs/content/docs/privacy.md`.
- **Collections — channel-scoped shared + den-wide non-E2EE personal.** Shared collections belong to one text channel (`everyone` adds or `staff` add, staff create/edit/delete, audited) and inherit its role visibility automatically. Personal collections span the den, hold channel, DM or Notes pointers, and store collection names sealed with the den's data key while DM message contents stay E2EE (opened via `denclient`'s existing `fetchKeys` + `open` path, which also registers per-message DM file keys). Bounds: 20 shared collections per text channel, 20 personal collections per member per den, 200 items per collection, names `1..50` runes.
- **Personal collection entries on role loss.** Options: hide while inaccessible and restore if access returns; permanently prune on role loss; show a redacted placeholder. Pick: *hide while inaccessible*, filtering both item queries and visible counts through `seesChannel` so lost-access entries leak neither text nor count and survive accidental role toggles; message deletion and retention still remove rows via `ON DELETE CASCADE`.
- **First personal collection (`Saved`).** Options: lazy default `Saved` shown in picker when a member has none; permanent built-in `Saved`; no default. Pick: *lazy default `Saved`*, giving one-click bookmarking with zero unused DB rows while allowing rename or deletion once created.
- **Channel collections view on desktop.** Options: replace the main pane (`← #channel`, scrollable tabs/pills, `+` for staff); right-side drawer beside chat. Pick: *replace the main pane*, sharing one full-width message layout across channel collections (`/den/<den>/channels/<ch>/collections`), personal collections (`/den/<den>/collections`) and the phone Back trail.
- **Steam card sizing and trailers — GO (verified in M25.0).** Separate Appearance setting (`DENS_STEAM_SIZE`, default Small = 400 px stacked; phone stays Small; Medium 640 / Large 960 / Largest 1280 place the picture/trailer beside the facts). Primary `all_ages: true` trailer from `IStoreBrowseService/GetItems` (`include_trailers: true`, `trailers.highlights[]`) plays on click in the picture box (zero bytes fetched until clicked; apps with `content_descriptorids` 3 or 4 like `1034140` mark trailers `all_ages: false` and any `Card.Adult` trailer stays behind the Adult cover; shares one-at-a-time playback with YouTube). Empirical probe (`spikes/steam-trailers`) across 14 live games (2004–2026) and Chromium 156, Firefox 153 ESR and Android 17 WebView 145 confirmed:
  - *Fixed CDN host and path validation:* all 152 live manifest and segment fetches succeeded with HTTP 200 and zero redirects from **`https://video.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_trailers/`**. Every `adaptive_trailers[].cdn_path` matches `^<appID>/[0-9]{1,12}/[0-9a-f]{40}/[0-9]{1,12}/dash_(h264|av1)\.mpd$`; every MPD uses relative `<SegmentTemplate>` paths `dash_(h264|av1)/init-stream<rep>.m4s` and `dash_(h264|av1)/chunk-stream<rep>-<5-digit-num>.m4s` (fixed 3.0 s chunks, `startNumber="1"`, no `BaseURL` or query). Sniff ISO-BMFF (`ftyp` for init, `styp`/`moof` for media); cap manifest at 64 KiB and single chunk at 4 MiB.
  - *Container, codecs and dual SourceBuffer:* fMP4 (`ftyp="iso5"`) with video in `AdaptationSet` 0 (`dash_h264`: `video/mp4; codecs="avc1.640029"`; `dash_av1`: `video/mp4; codecs="av01.0.05M.08..."`, up to 1080p/720p/480p/360p, older games lower e.g. 528p/394p) and audio always in a separate `AdaptationSet` 1 (`audio/mp4; codecs="mp4a.40.2"`, AAC-LC 192 kbps), requiring **two MSE `SourceBuffer`s** (`AdaptationSet` 2 `image/avif` thumbnails ignored). Prefer `dash_h264` with `dash_av1` fallback if `MediaSource.isTypeSupported` rejects `avc1`; both decoded cleanly in all three engines.
  - *CSP:* `video.srcObject = mediaSource` throws `TypeError` in all three engines (`MediaSourceHandle`/`MediaStream` only in Chromium/WebView; `MediaStream` only in Firefox), and `URL.createObjectURL(mediaSource)` is blocked by `media-src 'self'` in Chromium 156 and Android WebView 145 (`blockedURI: "blob"`). Widening only `media-src` in `PageSecurityHeaders` to **`media-src 'self' blob:`** plays cleanly with 0 CSP violations in all three engines (`script-src`, `connect-src`, `img-src`, `frame-src` unchanged).
  - *30 s byte weights and cache budget:* 480p + AAC = **4.7–5.9 MiB** (`dash_h264`, 2.7 MiB on 2007 Portal) / **2.5–6.5 MiB** (`dash_av1`); 720p + AAC = **8.3–10.3 MiB** (`dash_h264`, max chunk 1.36 MiB) / **4.0–11.8 MiB** (`dash_av1`, max chunk 2.31 MiB). Pick 480p on Small/phone and best rep ≤ 720p on Medium+. Proxy chunks through a dedicated **32 MiB** short-TTL (10 min) memory cache in `internal/steam`, separate from the 16 MiB card/image cache.
- **Stuck-unread fix.** Add additive `last_message_id` (`*string`) on `MessageDeleted`, populated inside every delete transaction (`Delete`, ban bulk delete, disappearing) via `SELECT coalesce(max(id), 0) FROM den_messages WHERE channel_id = ?`. `denclient` updates `reads[channel].LastMessage` and pushes updated read states on delete; the page also adds a fallback in `markRead()` for older dens so opening a channel whose `last_message_id` is newer than its newest remaining message (or empty) always clears unread state.
