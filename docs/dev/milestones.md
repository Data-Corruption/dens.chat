# Dens — Milestones

What each milestone built, and the plan for the one under way; ideas no milestone has taken up yet are in [loose-ideas.md](loose-ideas.md). The design they build toward is [design.md](design.md), with each deployment form's in [installed.md](installed.md), [flatpak.md](flatpak.md) and [android.md](android.md): a section named here, such as Global shortcuts, is one of theirs unless it's one of this file's.

A milestone's plan is written here first, and reviewed before anything is built. Once the milestone is done, what its plan still teaches, such as spike findings and measurements, moves into the design docs' sections, and the rest goes; Git keeps it.

Each milestone ends usable on its own and is tested on Linux and Windows, in Chromium and Firefox. As a rule, each step of a milestone is tested by the automated checks: the harnesses, the Android e2e in the emulator, and the browser checks in the container's Chromium and Firefox. The manual tests, on the phones and in browsers, run together once, at the milestone's end. A spike runs its own trials, and a test that can't wait for the end gets an exception. All platform-specific code lands in M0, while the seams are still cheap to change; later milestones are shared code, but for M7's keys and the helper's start at login, M9's Flatpak, M12's Android app and the helper's notifications, and M20's capture of a computer's sound.

**M21, fixes and the call timer.** M20 set aside six small things and the call timer, and members have reported five more since. M21 takes them all, and nothing that needs a new mechanism but the timer. Three of the reports had causes to find, found while planning:

- *The approval wait.* `waitForApproval` adds the sign-in without publishing the dens view, so Add a den shows its dots until the other device answers, and "Approve this on another device" shows only if something else publishes meanwhile.
- *The first frame behind a GIF.* On hover, an animated picture is drawn over its preview (`usePicture`'s `hover`), which shows through wherever the picture is transparent: avatars, banners, emoji and badges alike.
- *The bars' color.* `bg-bar` is `oklch(from var(--color-base-100) …)`, the stylesheet's one use of relative colors. On one theme the Android app's bar is `#1e231f` and Edge 158's, on a desktop, `#261f21`: the same lightness and chroma, the hue 151° in one and 359° in the other. Edge 158 is its dev channel's, a Chromium ahead of the WebView's, so the released ones may come to lose the hue too.

Left in loose-ideas.md: the phone as a computer's microphone, which is the relayed microphone M20 left out and wants a spike of its own; search, bookmarks and the rest.

M21.1:

- *The approval wait.* The service publishes the sign-in as it starts waiting, and the dialog takes it from the request's answer, so it never waits on the stream. The step then says it waits: "Waiting for your other device", with a spinner, over the line it has. Options for the device that approves: (a) as now, the request above Home and above that den's channel; (b) a notification too. Pick (a): the new device says where to look, and a notification would be a new kind with a setting of its own. The build checks that the phone layout shows the request on whichever pane is open.
- *A share's volume.* The mute button sets 0 and unmute sets 1, and every watch starts at 1. Options: (a) unmute returns to the volume before the mute, while the share is watched; (b) that, and the volume kept by den and sharer in the browser, as members' volumes are, so the next watch starts there. Pick (b): the store is there, and a loud sharer is loud every time.
- *The clock.* `continues` groups by author and time alone, and the clock sits in a run's header. A run ends where the next message disappears after a different time, or doesn't disappear, and where a line sits between two messages: the time set, a group's change, changed keys.
- *Animated pictures.* One component draws the preview and the file for avatars, banners, emoji and badges, and hides the preview once the file over it has loaded, so it still never blinks out as it starts.
- *A message's GIF.* Options: (a) as now, its preview in the list and the file in the viewer; (b) it plays while the pointer is over its message, whatever Animations says; (c) it follows Animations, as every other animated picture does: under Always it plays as it comes on screen, and under On hover while the pointer is over its message. Pick (c): one setting means one thing. A GIF can be as large as the den's files go, so under Always the page fetches one only as it nears the screen, and the cache keeps it. On a touch screen under On hover, a tap opens the viewer, as now.
- *The bars.* The checks' Chromium is older than the Edge that loses the hue and may not lose it yet, so the fix doesn't wait on showing it there. Options: (a) `color-mix(in oklab, var(--color-base-100), white 4%)`, which has no hue to lose and leaves a white page's bar white, as now; (b) the page computing each theme's bar and setting a variable. Pick (a): one line, in every browser DaisyUI 5 draws in. A light theme that isn't white gets a bar closer to its page than now.
- *Bangers.* A font's slanted letters reach past the name's box at its right, as tall ones reached past its top: the box grows sideways too, and takes no more room for it. The check measures every font's ink against its box.
- *An update's notice.* Home alone shows it, and a member with one den never opens Home. Options: (a) Home's notice above the channel in a den too, with its command or its button, and Dismiss, which the browser keeps for that version; (b) a dot on the settings' cog, and the notice in System. Pick (a): installing takes a command or a button, which a dot can't hold, and a dot goes unread. Home keeps showing it whatever was dismissed. On the phone layout it shows on whichever pane is open.
- *Interested.* Options: (a) as now, plain until marked and primary after; (b) primary until marked, then plain with a tick, "✓ Interested". Pick (b): the colored button is the one that asks for a click. The stars go.
- *Staff's marks.* Appearance's Messages gets Staff marks, off until the member turns it on: the crown and the shield beside names in messages and in a call's rows. The member list and the profile card always show them. The browser keeps it, as it keeps Density.
- *Hover.* Chat background gets Hover under Dim: how far a message under the pointer, or with its menu open, covers the picture, 0 to 100% in steps of 5, and 100 until set, as now. The service keeps it with the dim, so every paired page shows the same.

M21.2:

- *What it times.* A call, from its first member's join to its last one's leaving, a held call counting. Options: (a) the whole call, alone or not; (b) only while two or more are in it. Pick (a): a timer that stops while someone waits alone reads as broken. One member idling over a weekend sets a record nobody beats, so staff reset it.
- *The den.* A call's room keeps when it began, and `voice.state` and `ready` carry each call's `elapsed_ms` and `record_ms`, the channel's record as the call began. Both are additive: an older client ignores them, and an older den sends neither, so its calls show no timer. The record is a column on the channel, by a migration, written as a call ends longer than it, and each minute a call runs past it, so a restart loses a minute at most. The den keeps the length alone, not who was in the call or when.
- *Clocks.* The member's service turns `elapsed_ms` into a start on its own clock as the frame arrives, so a den's clock and a computer's can disagree without the timer showing it.
- *The row.* Every voice channel with a call shows its length at the row's right, to everyone who sees the channel: `m:ss`, then `h:mm:ss`, in figures of one width, from one timer in the page. Its tip names the record. The lock leaves the row. Options for it: (a) the call's bar and pane keep theirs, where every member in the call has it; (b) the channel's settings dialog too. Pick (a): that dialog is staff's alone.
- *The record.* While a call is longer than `record_ms`, its length shows as a moving rainbow; as it passes, fireworks play over the row for about two seconds, on every page showing the row then. A page that opens later shows the rainbow alone. A channel with no record yet shows neither: there's nothing to beat. Both are the stylesheet's, with no library; under `prefers-reduced-motion` the fireworks don't play and the rainbow holds still.
- *Reset.* The channel's dialog shows staff its longest call, with Reset, which a confirmation guards since it's everyone's.
- *The setting.* Appearance's Call timer, on until the member turns it off, which hides the length, the rainbow and the fireworks. The browser keeps it.
