# Dens — Milestones

What each milestone built, and the plan for the one under way; ideas no milestone has taken up yet are in [loose-ideas.md](loose-ideas.md). The design they build toward is [design.md](design.md), with each deployment form's in [installed.md](installed.md), [flatpak.md](flatpak.md) and [android.md](android.md): a section named here, such as Global shortcuts, is one of theirs unless it's one of this file's.

A milestone's plan is written here first, and reviewed before anything is built. Once the milestone is done, what its plan still teaches, such as spike findings and measurements, moves into the design docs' sections, and the rest goes; Git keeps it.

Each milestone ends usable on its own and is tested on Linux and Windows, in Chromium and Firefox. As a rule, each step of a milestone is tested by the automated checks: the harnesses, the Android e2e in the emulator, and the browser checks in the container's Chromium and Firefox. The manual tests, on the phones and in browsers, run together once, at the milestone's end. A spike runs its own trials, and a test that can't wait for the end gets an exception. All platform-specific code lands in M0, while the seams are still cheap to change; later milestones are shared code, but for M7's keys and the helper's start at login, M9's Flatpak, and M12's Android app and the helper's notifications.

**M19, cosmetics.** Since v1.0.0 every milestone has added to the settings, a card at a time, and their words were last read as a whole in M8.3. M19 reads them again, lays them out as Manage is, and takes up what members noticed that needs no new mechanism: marks at two heights, a slider that drags its menu along, a Short in a landscape frame, names cut off, the browser's menu where the page has one of its own. With them go the voice asks that came in during M18, and custom themes, which members have wanted since the theme picker. Custom themes looked like a rabbit hole; they aren't, scoped as below: a DaisyUI theme is a fixed set of CSS variables the page already draws every control from, and the page can set them at runtime under its CSP, so a theme of the member's own is those variables kept in the browser, with nothing new in the stylesheet, and no drawing of a color picker. The rabbit holes are left out: a picture behind every surface, which would take every pane, sheet and menu translucent; a color picker the page draws; and a theme that reaches other members, beyond a name's style, which is M19.5's. Search, bookmarks and the rest stay in loose-ideas.md.

M19.2:

M19.3:

- *Louder.* A call member's slider runs to 200%, 100 marked on it, which multiplies their gain; above 100 a loud voice may clip, which the member hears and turns down. Call volume stays at most 100. The browser keeps volumes above 1, which a page from before M19.3 reads as 100%.
- *Release delay.* Under Push to talk, Release delay, 0 to 2,000 ms in steps of 50, 0 unless set, so nothing changes for members who don't touch it. The gate stays open, and the ring stays lit, for the delay after the key's release, whether the page or the helper saw it, and a press within the delay keeps it open. The call's bar already deafens and shows it.
- *Moderators moving moderators.* Moving between calls gets a rule of its own: staff move anyone but the owner and themselves; mute for everyone and disconnect keep theirs, that staff act on those they outrank. The page's Move to and the drag follow it. No other permission changes.

M19.4:

- *A theme of one's own.* Under More themes, Your themes: New theme opens an editor in the card. It starts from the theme the page shows, and asks for a name, Light or Dark, five colors, the background, the text, primary, secondary and accent, each a color input with its hex beside it, and the corners, square, rounded or round. The rest derives: the two deeper backgrounds a step of lightness from the first, as `bg-bar` steps now, each color's own text light or dark, whichever contrasts more, and the neutral, info, success, warning and error colors from the theme it started from, since they carry meaning, which the theme keeps as its own. The page takes the theme as the member edits, and Cancel puts the one before back. Options for the picker: the browser's `<input type="color">`, or one the page draws; the browser's, with the hex field beside it the same everywhere, since a picker of the page's own is the rabbit hole.
- *Where it lives.* A custom theme is its variables as the page sets them, kept by the browser as the chosen theme is, up to 20, and chosen as the others are, with its swatch in the grid; Edit and Delete on the chosen one. The page applies it by setting the variables on the document's root through the CSSOM, which the CSP allows as it allows the styles the page sets now, over Light or Dark for what a theme leaves to DaisyUI; the share's window takes the same, as it takes the theme's name; and the Android app's bars follow its background as they follow any theme's.
- *Sharing.* Copy code gives `dens-theme:` and the theme's fields as hex digits, dots between, which mean nothing to a message's markdown; Paste a code takes one. A code is a member's message, so it's hostile: the page reads it whole by one pattern, a name of up to 40 characters, Light or Dark, the corners by letter, and ten colors of six hex digits each, and refuses anything else, so nothing but a color the page wrote out ever reaches a style. The canvas reads only the page's own colors, the theme a new one starts from.
- *A picture behind the chat.* Appearance's Chat background: Choose image, Dim, from 0 to 90%, and Remove. The picture shows behind the message list in every channel, DM and group, under a scrim of the theme's background at the dim, and nowhere else. Options for keeping it: (a) the member's Dens keeps it, as it keeps a sound they replaced (M14.2): stripped by `internal/media` as a banner is, up to 10 MB, PNG, JPEG or WebP, in the database under the data key, served to the page at a client route sandboxed as sounds are, and in backups; (b) the browser keeps it, with the theme, in IndexedDB. Pick (a): a path the page and the service already have, shared by every browser paired with this Dens, and nothing new on the page but a card. The dim goes with it in the settings the service keeps, 70% until set, and the dens view tells every page both. An animated PNG or WebP is refused, as a GIF is.
- *Names.* What reaches other members is M19.5's, and nothing in a theme does.

- *Reading on every theme.* A color a member picked over their theme may vanish on another's, so the page checks each name's color against the background it sits on, the list's base-200 or the messages' base-100, as it draws it. Options for one that falls under 3:1: (a) the page's default color in its place; (b) the color nudged in OKLCH, its lightness moved toward the text's side a step at a time until it passes, its hue kept, so a dark red on a dark theme reads as a lighter red. Pick (b): the member's choice survives every theme, and nobody wonders where it went. Edit profile says in a line that the color may lighten or darken on others' themes.
- *The fonts.* A set of eight display faces, Latin alone, each a Fontsource package pinned by version and SHA-256 in `vendor.sh` and copied at build into the page's assets, the Latin woff2 of one weight each, 15 to 30 KB apiece, served from the binary as the page's other assets are, under `font-src 'self'`; a browser loads a face only as a name on screen uses it. A name in another script falls through to the page's font, and the picker says so in a line. No other effects: Discord's neon, toon and the rest stay out.
