# Dens — Milestones

What each milestone built, and the plan for the one under way; ideas no milestone has taken up yet are in [loose-ideas.md](loose-ideas.md). The design they build toward is [design.md](design.md), with each deployment form's in [installed.md](installed.md), [flatpak.md](flatpak.md) and [android.md](android.md): a section named here, such as Global shortcuts, is one of theirs unless it's one of this file's.

A milestone's plan is written here first, and reviewed before anything is built. Once the milestone is done, what its plan still teaches, such as spike findings and measurements, moves into the design docs' sections, and the rest goes; Git keeps it.

Each milestone ends usable on its own and is tested on Linux and Windows, in Chromium and Firefox. As a rule, each step of a milestone is tested by the automated checks: the harnesses, the Android e2e in the emulator, and the browser checks in the container's Chromium and Firefox. The manual tests, on the phones and in browsers, run together once, at the milestone's end. A spike runs its own trials, and a test that can't wait for the end gets an exception. All platform-specific code lands in M0, while the seams are still cheap to change; later milestones are shared code, but for M7's keys and the helper's start at login, M9's Flatpak, and M12's Android app and the helper's notifications.

M17.1:

- *The install guide.* Options: (a) Hextra's tabs, which the site already syncs, so one choice turns every step's tabs; (b) one set of tabs at the top, each holding a platform's whole guide; (c) a page for each platform, behind a choice at `/docs/install/`. Pick (c): a reader picks once, then reads one page top to bottom with nothing of the other platforms in it, and no script is needed. `/docs/install/` holds four cards, Windows, Linux, Linux by the Flatpak (for NixOS, SteamOS and the others the installer refuses) and Android, under a line on what Dens is and above a line each on what it runs on. Windows', Linux's and the Flatpak's pages (`/docs/install/windows/`, `/linux/` and `/flatpak/`) each run: what it needs, download, run, and open Dens with its local password, then updating and removing at the foot. Android's card opens its guide. Sending logs, the same everywhere, moves to a page of its own.
- *Words.* A step is a line or two of what to do; why, and what to check, fold under it in Hextra's details, for whoever wants them. Checking a download's signature stays optional, and folded. The Join and Android guides get the same pass where they run long, keeping their headings.
- *Links.* The guides' links to the old sections move with them. A released Dens links `/docs/install/#the-flatpak` from the installer's refusal, which now lands on the choice, the Flatpak's card in it; the binary's link moves to the Flatpak's page. The page's links into the other guides keep working.
- *The landing table.* Calls end-to-end encrypted becomes Yes for Dens, and Who can read your group chats says the den reads channels while only a group DM's members read it. The other apps' cells are checked against their own pages, and the note's date with them.
- *CI.* Every PR runs the Release workflow, but its plan chooses the jobs by the files the PR changes, its merge commit against the first parent, in `ci.sh` rather than the workflow's YAML. Only `docs/` and the root's `.md` files but `CHANGELOG.md`, which releases are cut from: no test job. The media module's inputs (`internal/media/`, `scripts/ffmpeg.sh`, `scripts/rnnoise.sh`, `internal/ui/rnnoise/`, RNNoise's module, `scripts/vendor.sh` and `scripts/build/common.sh`) or CI's own files (`.github/`, `scripts/ci.sh` and `scripts/ci/`): everything. Anything else: everything but `media-module` and `media-fuzz`, which check the media module alone. A list it can't read: everything. Pushes to main plan as now, so a release still runs every job, as its job requires. A path filter on the workflow could only skip all of it, and a workflow it filters out leaves required checks waiting where a skipped job passes. The release harness tests each case.
- *The site on PRs.* The Docs workflow builds the site on every PR that touches it, as `hugo --panicOnWarning`, and runs the one test that reads it (the site's icon is the mark), deploying from main alone. `./scripts/test.sh -docs` does the same locally.

M17.2:

- *Compact.* Appearance's Message density: Default, as now, or Compact, which shows each message as a line of its time, its author's name and its text, which wraps under the name. A reply's quote sits above, and its files, players, polls and reactions below, from the name's start. Every message shows its name, not only a run's first, and none its author's picture. Phones get it too, with the same long press. The browser keeps the choice, as it keeps Animations.
- *Dragging channels.* On a desktop, staff drag a channel to another place in its group or into another group, and a group among the groups. The drop sends what Move up, Move down and Group send now, the channel's new place in its list, which the den already takes. Touch screens and keyboards keep the settings' Move up and Move down. It's HTML's drag and drop, by which the message pane already takes files.
- *Moving a member.* On a desktop, staff drag a member's row in a call onto another voice channel; anywhere, the member's menu in the call has Move to, listing the den's voice channels. The den checks what disconnecting checks, that the staff member outranks them, and what joining does, that the member sees the channel and it has room, then sends `voice.move` to the socket holding the member's call. Their page joins that channel as if they'd picked it, sealed, muted and deafened as they were, which ends the old call; the den doesn't count that join toward the member's limit on joining. A call held while its socket is away can't be told, so the move is refused (`409 call_held`), and a Dens from before M17.2, which ignores the event, stays where it is.
- *Spoilers.* A photo or video waiting in the composer has Spoiler, beside Send full size. One sent as a spoiler shows covered, its preview blurred under Spoiler, until it's clicked or tapped, each time its message is shown, and nothing plays before. The send names which of its files are spoilers: in a channel the den keeps it on the file (`spoiler`); in a DM or a group it goes inside the seal with the file's name, so the den never learns it. Once sent, it stays. An older Dens shows them uncovered.
- *Check now.* The Android app's System settings, in their Updates card, have Check now, whatever the daily checks are set to: the service asks the release host at once, and downloads and checks a newer release as the daily check does before it answers, so the card says Dens is up to date, or that the update is ready, with Install beside it. An installed Dens has none: `dens update` checks as it runs.

M17.3:

- *Notes.* Each member has notes in each den: a conversation with themselves, pinned at the top of the Direct messages tab, and opened from Notes on their own profile card. Text, files, replies, edits and deletes work as in a DM; reactions, polls, mentions and disappearing times don't, nor notifications or unread marks, since everything in it is theirs. The den's retention, and the member's upload limit, apply as to a DM.
- *Their key.* Options: (a) 32 random bytes sealed to the member's own identity, kept on the den as a DM's key is; (b) a key made from the DM seal by HKDF, salted with the den's ID under a label of its own, as the identity is. Pick (b): every device holding the seal has it, with nothing to make, keep or hand over, and the den holds nothing of it. Messages name it by the channel's ID and are sealed as a DM's are, binding the den, the channel, the member, the nonce, the key and the revision; each file is sealed under a key of its own, carried in the sealed text, as in a DM.
- *Starting over.* A member who starts over has a new seal, so their old notes no longer open, as their old DMs don't: they show as lost, for the member to delete.
- *On the den.* A channel of a new kind, `notes`, naming its member, one for each, made as they first open it (`POST /api/notes` answers with it, and the same one after), which only they see. It takes sealed messages alone. `den_channels` is made again to take the kind, as M16.3 made it for group DMs.
- *Protocol 3.* A Dens from before M17.3 drops its connection on a channel of a kind it doesn't know, so the den raises the protocol to 3, serves 1 to 3, and sends notes only to sockets speaking 3, as it sends groups only to those speaking 2: in `ready`, in events, in history and in the change log. A member's devices still speaking 2 show nothing of their notes until they update. The page offers Notes once its Dens and the den both speak 3.

M17.4:

- *The Events row.* At the top of the Den tab's list, Events, with how many are to come, or Live while one runs, opens the den's events in the main pane: those live first, then the rest by start. An ended event leaves it. The pane's + Event makes one. The den's name still opens Manage: events are every member's, and Manage mostly staff's.
- *An event.* A name (100 characters), a description in the markdown subset (2,000), an optional banner, cropped 3:1 and checked as a profile banner is, a start, in the member's time zone and up to a year ahead, a length from 5 minutes to a week, and a place: one of the den's voice channels, or a line of text. One in a staff-only channel shows to staff alone, and follows its channel if that changes.
- *Who makes them.* The owner's setting, in Manage's Limits: how many events each member may have to come, 0 by default, which leaves events to staff, up to 10. A den holds 50 to come in all. Members edit and cancel their own; staff edit and cancel those of members they outrank, by the rule calls and messages use. A member's events to come go when they leave the den.
- *Live.* From its start to its end. Its maker or staff end it early, with End now, or move its end, never before now and within its length's limits. One that has started can't be canceled, only ended. The den's timer, which ends polls and deletes disappearing messages, starts and ends events.
- *Interested.* Each member's mark, on the event and on its card (M17.5). Members see how many, and their own mark; the den keeps who's interested, to tell them, shows nobody the list, and forgets it as the event ends.
- *Starting.* As an event starts, the den sends `event.started` to its interested members, numbered like other events, so a socket that resumes soon after still hears it. Their Dens notifies them under Events, a new kind in the notification settings, on by default, and a tap opens the event. The den pushes it, since Go's timers stop while a phone sleeps; on Android it comes as messages' notifications do, and the shell opens an event's path. A member whose Dens wasn't connected hears nothing later. Not planned: repeating events, reminders before the start, and answers beyond Interested.
- *The protocol.* Additive: the den's info gains `event_limits`, which says the den has events; `ready` carries the events to come and live that the member sees, with their own mark; events tell of one made, changed, canceled, started and ended, and of its count; and requests make, change, cancel and end one, and mark Interested and take it back. An older Dens ignores them. Each Dens keeps the events with the den's state, so they show offline.

M17.5:

- *An event's link.* Copy link on an event gives `<den URL>/#e/<den ID>/<event>`, as a message's is `#m/`. In a message, a link to an event in a den the member is in shows a card: its banner, its name, when, in the member's time zone, where, how many are interested, and Interested. A card whose event isn't among the den's to come asks the den for it, and an ended one says so; one the member can't see, or in a den they're not in, stays a link. The links' shared cases take event links.
- *Badges.* Staff attach one to an event in a voice channel while making or editing it: a square picture, checked as an emoji is, and a title of up to 40 characters. It's kept as one of the den's named files, of kind `badge`, which every member opens, as they do an emoji. The kind needs no new check.
- *Earning one.* A member in the event's call for a minute at a stretch while it's live, their call held or not, earns its badge, once. The den times each stretch in memory, keeps only the award, the badge, the member and when, and forgets the stretches as the event ends, so it keeps no record of who was in the call.
- *Showing them.* A member's den profile card shows their badges in a row, each with a tip: its title, its event's name and its date. Members' records carry their badges, and `ready` the den's, each with its event's name and date, so a badge outlives its event's place in the list.

The migrations follow M16.4's, one for each step that needs one: M17.2's adds a file's `spoiler`; M17.3's makes `den_channels` again to take notes, as M16.3's did for group DMs; M17.4's adds events, who's interested in them, and the den's `member_events`; and M17.5's adds badges and their awards. M17.1 needs none. M17.3 raises the protocol to 3; the rest of M17's protocol is additive.

Each step's docs land with it: M17.1's in the public site, design.md's Public site, release.md's CI jobs and AGENTS.md's Build and test; M17.2's in design.md's Den features, Files and media, and Voice and screen share, protocol.md's Attachments and Voice, and the public join.md and host.md; M17.3's in design.md's End-to-end encryption and Versioning and compatibility, protocol.md's Versioning and a Notes section beside Private DMs, and the public join.md and privacy.md; and M17.4's and M17.5's in an Events section of design.md's Den features, an Events section of protocol.md, android.md's Notifications, and the public join.md and host.md.
