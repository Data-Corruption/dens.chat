# Dens — Milestones

What each milestone built, and the plan for the one under way; ideas no milestone has taken up yet are in [loose-ideas.md](loose-ideas.md). The design they build toward is [design.md](design.md), with each deployment form's in [installed.md](installed.md), [flatpak.md](flatpak.md) and [android.md](android.md): a section named here, such as Global shortcuts, is one of theirs unless it's one of this file's.

A milestone's plan is written here first, and reviewed before anything is built. Once the milestone is done, what its plan still teaches, such as spike findings and measurements, moves into the design docs' sections, and the rest goes; Git keeps it.

Each milestone ends usable on its own and is tested on Linux and Windows, in Chromium and Firefox. As a rule, each step of a milestone is tested by the automated checks: the harnesses, the Android e2e in the emulator, and the browser checks in the container's Chromium and Firefox. The manual tests, on the phones and in browsers, run together once, at the milestone's end. A spike runs its own trials, and a test that can't wait for the end gets an exception. All platform-specific code lands in M0, while the seams are still cheap to change; later milestones are shared code, but for M7's keys and the helper's start at login, M9's Flatpak, and M12's Android app and the helper's notifications.

M17 gets its plan as it begins, from what is set aside for it below.

**M17, set aside: events and badges.** Weighed beside M15, events and their badges wait for M17, after M16's end-to-end encryption, and get the rest of their plan as M17 begins. Settled so far:

- An Events row at the top of a den's channel list, with how many are coming up, or Live while one runs, opens the den's events. The den's name still opens Manage: events are every member's, and Manage mostly staff's.
- An event has a name, a description in the markdown subset, an optional banner (a profile banner's 3:1 crop), a start, a length, and a place: a voice channel, or a line of text. One in a staff-only channel shows to staff alone.
- Who makes events is the owner's setting: staff alone, by default, or every member, with a cap on each member's events to come. Staff edit and cancel those of members of a lower rank.
- Interested is a member's mark, counted on the event. As the event starts, the den tells the services of those interested, which notify them under a new kind, Events: the den pushes it, since Go's timers stop while a phone sleeps. The timer that ends polls (M15.4) grows to start and end events.
- An event's link, copied from it as a message's is, shows in a message as a card drawn from the den's state: when, in the member's time zone, where, how many are interested, and Interested.
- Badges: staff attach one to an event in a voice channel, a square image and a title, kept as one of the den's named files, of kind `badge`. Being in that call while the event is live earns it; the den keeps the award, never who was in the call. A den profile card shows a member's badges, each with its title, its event and the date in a tip.
- Not planned: repeating events, reminders before the start, and answers beyond Interested.
