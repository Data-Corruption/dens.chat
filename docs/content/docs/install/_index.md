Dens is chat for your friends or community, and everyone in it runs Dens on
their own computer or phone. Pick yours:

{{< cards cols="2" >}}
  {{< card link="windows/" title="Windows" icon="desktop-computer" >}}
  {{< card link="linux/" title="Linux" icon="terminal" >}}
  {{< card link="flatpak/" title="Linux by the Flatpak" icon="cube" subtitle="For NixOS, SteamOS and the others the installer refuses." >}}
  {{< card link="../android/" title="Android" icon="device-mobile" >}}
{{< /cards >}}

- **Windows**: Windows 11, version 24H2 or later, Home or Pro.
- **Linux**: Fedora (Workstation and its spins), Bazzite, Debian 12 or later,
  Ubuntu 24.04 or later and the distributions built on it, such as Linux Mint
  and Pop!\_OS, Arch, and CachyOS.
- **The Flatpak**: any other Linux, such as NixOS or SteamOS. It joins dens,
  but can't host one.
- **Android**: Android 12 or later. The app joins dens, but can't host one.

Linux keeps Dens more tightly locked down than Windows can; [Privacy](../privacy/#linux-and-windows)
has the details.
